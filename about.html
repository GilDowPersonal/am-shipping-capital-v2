About this concept.

This site was put together ahead of a meeting, to sketch a possible website and AI direction for Am Shipping Capital.

Herzliya, Israel is the location provided for this concept. The business's legal identity, its connection to that location and its official details have not been independently verified, and nothing here should be read as confirming them.

You are looking at the second version: a completely new design and new copy for the same idea.

The AI applications described on this site are proposals. No company systems are connected, and no live agents run here.

Design notes

A harbour palette.

  • Ink#0B1014
  • Deep#12303D
  • Fog#EEF1EF
  • Signal#F0642E
  • Steel#5E6B73
  • Mist#A9B7BD

Dark water, harbour fog and the orange of a signal light. Type uses the fonts already on your device, and the site loads no tracking, remote assets or external scripts. These colours are a proposal for discussion, not company-approved brand standards.

Honesty notes

What this site does not claim.

  • There are no testimonials here because none have been independently verified. That is not a claim that no reviews of the company exist.
  • It offers no official contact details, company history, guarantees or partnerships.
  • It takes no bookings or shipment requests, and it shows no invented results or savings.
  • An official version would need a verified identity, accurate information, and branding and claims approved by the company.
