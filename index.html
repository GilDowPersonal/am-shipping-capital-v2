01 · The route

Every shipment is a chain of handovers.

A single consignment can pass from a truck to a terminal, onto a vessel, and on to its destination. The container is what lets all of those parties work with one shared unit, and ports are where the sea leg is stitched to the land leg.

Each of those transfers is also a moment where the paperwork has to match what is physically happening. This concept is built around the people who manage those moments — and around giving them better information, not taking their place.

02 · The concept

Rules, reasoning, and a human hand on the helm.

  • 01

    Automation

    Fixed rules carry structured data between approved systems, checking it at every handover.

  • 02

    AI assistance

    Models read untidy documents, pull out the details that matter and summarise them — always pointing back to the source.

  • 03

    Bounded agents

    Agents link a few tightly scoped tool steps together, while people sign off on commitments and anything sensitive.
